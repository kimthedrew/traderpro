1. What traderpro is

traderpro is a third-party application that connects to your own Deriv account via Deriv's OAuth2 login. traderpro is not Deriv, is not affiliated with or endorsed by Deriv, and does not hold, custody, or have direct access to your funds. All trades execute on the Deriv account you already control and have authorized.

2. Eligibility

You must have your own Deriv account in good standing, and be legally permitted to trade the products Deriv offers in your jurisdiction. It is your responsibility to know and follow the laws that apply to you. [Jurisdiction-specific eligibility restrictions to be added after legal review.]

3. Non-custodial access

traderpro connects to your Deriv account through Deriv's own OAuth2 authorization flow. You explicitly grant traderpro a limited set of permissions (currently: trading operations and account management) each time you log in. You can revoke this access at any time from your Deriv account's own settings. traderpro never sees or stores your Deriv password.

4. Signals

Signals are automated alerts generated from public market data using a simple, published trigger (see the Signals section on the homepage for the current rule). Signals are informational only. They are not financial advice, a recommendation to trade, or a guarantee of any outcome. Past signal activity is not indicative of future results. You are solely responsible for any trading decisions you make.

5. Copy Trading — currently shadow mode only

As of this draft, Copy Trading does not place real trades. It computes and logs what a trade copied from a configured leader account would have looked like for your account, based on the stake ratio and optional maximum stake you set. Nothing executes against your real Deriv account through this feature at this time.

If and when live trade execution is introduced, it will require a separate, explicit opt-in, additional terms specific to that risk, and will not be silently enabled for existing shadow-mode users. When that happens: copy trading, like all trading, carries risk of loss; a leader's past performance does not predict future results; and traderpro is not responsible for losses arising from copied trades.

6. No guarantee of profit

Trading derivatives, including the synthetic indices and options Deriv offers, carries a high risk of loss. Nothing in traderpro — signals, copy trading, or any future feature — guarantees profit or protects against loss. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

7. Account security

You are responsible for keeping your Deriv account credentials secure. traderpro uses an httpOnly session cookie to keep you logged in to traderpro itself; if you use a shared or public device, log out when you're done.
