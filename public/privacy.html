Privacy Policy

Draft — no effective date yet.

1. What we collect

  • Your Deriv account ID (loginid) and currency, once you log in.
  • An OAuth2 access token for your Deriv account, used server-side only — it never reaches your browser.
  • If you configure Copy Trading: your stake ratio, optional max stake, and a log of what would have been copied to your account (see Copy Trading — currently shadow mode only, nothing here reflects real trades).
  • Standard web server request logs (e.g. IP address), used only for rate-limiting abuse and debugging — not analytics.

2. What we don't collect

We never see or store your Deriv password. We don't request more account permissions (OAuth scopes) than the app currently uses. We don't run any analytics or advertising trackers. We don't collect payment card details — traderpro doesn't process payments.

3. How we use it

To keep you logged in (a session cookie identifies your session; the underlying account data lives server-side), to show your account info in the interface, and — if you use Copy Trading — to compute and display your shadow-copy log.

4. Data retention

Sessions currently last up to 24 hours or until you log out, whichever comes first. Logging out clears your session on traderpro's side, but does not revoke the underlying Deriv authorization — revoke that separately from your Deriv account settings if you want to fully disconnect. 5. Third parties

Deriv, as the account/OAuth provider — necessary for the app to function. A database hosting provider (chosen separately — see the project's README) stores account and session data. No data is sold or shared for advertising.

6. Cookies

One httpOnly session cookie, used solely to keep you logged in. No marketing or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Your Deriv access token is never sent to your browser. Login attempts are rate-limited. See the project's public source code for the actual implementation.
